We are a genuine Peruvian Restaurant & Catering located in the heart of East Los Angeles serving flavorful cuisine, using only fresh ingredients. The dynamic couple Caroline and Ralph serve a variety of tasty dishes in a cozy ambiance including Ceviche Clasico, Signature Lomo Saltado, Peruvian-style fried rice, and homemade salsas. The food comes from our hearts and our generational family recipes. It is very important for us to pass on these cultural riches to the world around us.
