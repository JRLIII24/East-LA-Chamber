The East Los Angeles Community Youth Center (ELACYC) is a non-profit organization that offers hands-on classes, labs, and fun activities in fields of STEAM (Science, Engineering, Technology, Art & Math). We do these through our afterschool program, all-day seasonal camps, and Saturday classes. Additionally, we provide homework assistance, and tutoring to all our members that need that extra helping hand.
