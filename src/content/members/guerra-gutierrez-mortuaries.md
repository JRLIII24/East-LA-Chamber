Guerra & Gutierrez is not just any mortuary. It carries our families’ names and that legacy pushes us to be better. What the Guerra & Gutierrez families started decades ago-What we stand for-is still part of the business today. From our very first day, we have been a family run mortuary serving families of all faiths. Since our founding in 1945, the Guerra & Gutierrez families have always believed in fair prices for funeral services. We want the families we serve to have the very best services at a cost they can afford. A family owned firm will give you the personal attention and caring that you deserve, along with the extra support that you will need after the funeral is over. You will receive outstanding professionalism that only generations of service can cultivate.
