Michoacana in the Heart of East LA is a locally owned and operated ice cream parlor in East Los Angeles, CA. The state of Michoacán inspired our ice cream with their unique flavors, incredible textures, and overall deliciousness. Additionally, we also make our bubble waffles fresh every day! Our frozen and sweet treats will surely delight your taste buds. Our priority is to serve inexpensive but delicious desserts and snacks. We serve various Mexican snacks, including crepes, raspados, mangonadas, aguas frescas, esquites, and paletas. You will indeed have a great time when you visit us! We only use the best locally sourced ingredients to make everything from scratch. We are currently hosting a special Taco Tuesday promo. Every Tuesday, if you buy our taco ice cream, you can buy a second one for half price. To explore our fantastic selection of ice cream, visit us today at Michoacana in the Heart of East LA!
