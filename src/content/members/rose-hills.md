Comprehensive and Compassionate Funeral, Mortuary, and Cemetery Services at Rose Hills

For generations, Rose Hills has provided dignified and comprehensive care for families in Southern California. Our breathtaking 1,400-acre memorial park, the largest in North America, offers a serene and beautiful final resting place. We are honored to provide a complete range of services to meet the unique needs of every family, all at one location.

**Our services include:**

**Funeral and Memorial Services:** We offer personalized funeral and memorial services to honor the life of your loved one. Our experienced and multilingual staff is dedicated to serving families from all cultural and religious backgrounds, with special expertise in a wide array of traditions. - **Mortuary Care:** Our on-site, full-service mortuary provides professional and compassionate care, including embalming, preparation, and viewing services in our peaceful and comfortable staterooms. - **Cremation Services:** We offer a variety of cremation options, including traditional services with viewing and an on-site crematory, ensuring your loved one is always in our dedicated care. - **Cemetery Property:** Choose from a wide selection of interment options, including traditional ground burial, mausoleum entombment, cremation niches, and private estates, all within our meticulously maintained gardens and landscapes. - **Pre-Planning:** Secure peace of mind for yourself and your family by making arrangements in advance. Our counselors can guide you through the process, ensuring your final wishes are understood and respected.

With a variety of chapels to accommodate services of all sizes and a commitment to honoring diverse traditions, Rose Hills is dedicated to providing a seamless and supportive experience during a difficult time. Our team is available 24/7 to assist you.
