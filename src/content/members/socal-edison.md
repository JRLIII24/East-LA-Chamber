Southern California Edison: Powering Your Community

Southern California Edison (SCE) is dedicated to safely delivering reliable and affordable electricity to homes and businesses across the region. We are committed to a clean energy future, investing in innovative technologies and renewable resources to power your life sustainably.

Our core services include:

Reliable Electricity Supply: Count on us to keep your lights on and your businesses running with dependable power.Customer Support: We offer convenient ways to manage your account, pay your bill, and access assistance when you need it, both online and over the phone.Outage Assistance: Our dedicated crews work around the clock to restore power quickly and safely during outages, with resources available to keep you informed.Energy Efficiency Programs: Discover ways to save energy and money with our rebates, incentives, and tips for homes and businesses.Clean Energy Initiatives: We are actively working to integrate more renewable energy sources, support electric vehicle adoption, and build a smarter, more resilient grid for a cleaner tomorrow.

Whether you're a residential customer, a small business, or a large commercial enterprise, SCE is your trusted partner for essential energy services, striving to power your present and illuminate a brighter future.

Powering Southern California: Your Energy Partner

Southern California Edison (SCE) is dedicated to safely delivering reliable, affordable, and clean electricity to homes and businesses across the region. We are committed to a sustainable energy future for California, empowering our customers with innovative solutions and a wide range of services.

For Your Home:

Reliable Electricity Service: Count on us to keep your lights on. We offer various rate plans, including Time-Of-Use options, to help you manage your energy costs effectively.Customer Support & Bill Management: Easily start, stop, or transfer service. Access convenient online tools to pay your bill, understand your energy usage, and explore assistance programs.Energy Efficiency & Savings: Discover rebates and incentives for energy-efficient appliances, smart thermostats, and home improvements. Our programs are designed to help you save money and conserve energy.Electric Vehicle (EV) Support: Get information and resources for making the switch to an EV, including specialized rate plans and potential rebates.Outage Assistance: Report outages easily through our Outage Center, get updates on restoration times, and find tips to prepare for power interruptions.

For Your Business:

Tailored Energy Solutions: We provide a variety of rate plans and programs designed to meet the unique energy needs of small, medium, and large businesses.Energy Management Tools: Access resources and expertise to help your business improve energy efficiency, reduce operational costs, and achieve sustainability goals.Dedicated Support: Our team is here to assist with service requests, billing inquiries, and guidance on energy-saving opportunities.Powering Growth & Innovation: We support economic development and partner with businesses to implement clean energy solutions, including EV charging infrastructure.

Committed to a Clean Energy Future:

SCE is at forefront of California's transition to a clean energy economy. We are actively investing in grid modernization, expanding renewable energy sources like solar and wind, and promoting energy storage solutions. Together, we can create a more sustainable and resilient Southern California.
