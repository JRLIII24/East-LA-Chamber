Tacos Baja is a quick spot to relax and eat Ensenada style dishes. Popular choices range from fish tacos, shrimp tacos, shrimp cocktails and ceviche tostadas, all distinctly accompanied by roasted yellow “chiles gueros” lightly sprinkled by red chilly powder. Our popularity has captured the attention of serval well known magazine & newspapers and they all agree that Tacos Baja is a must do event in Southern California. The 25 Best Mexican Restaurants by Patric Kub in his Los Angeles Magazine.
