Community Responsibility is the heart of our business believes. At Tamayo & Associates, we seek to be known much more for our commitment to our communities and for the quality of our local real estate services. Our Local Responsibility program incorporates Professional Services Local Charities Community Outreach Ethics and Compliance Relationship Building Problem Solving It is our commitment to do the right thing and to act responsibly in our day-to-day relationships with our customers, communities and all our Associates. We focus our attention on the areas where we believe we can make the greatest difference by using our talent, good spirit and resources to impact the quality of our communities and the lives of others; and helping our agents to reach their full potential while providing a safe and ethical workplace. Our responsible business practices are an essential element of our brand and fundamental to our core values of service to our community and responsibility to our clients.
