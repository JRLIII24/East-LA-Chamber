Tom’s Burgers is a real veteran of Los Angeles restaurant scene. For more than 58 years we are trying to make our food consistently good. It is simple and straightforward classic burgers, hotdogs, pastrami sandwiches, chile fries and a strong Mexican specialties selection. If you walked in off East Los Angeles streets to our place you will immediately find our menu to be reasonably priced, service to be quick, and a good fresh food.
