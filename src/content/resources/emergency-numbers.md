For immediate life-threatening emergencies, **always dial 911**.Below is a list of important emergency and urgent contact numbers for services in the East Los Angeles area:

- **East Los Angeles Sheriff’s Station (Urgent Matters):**

  - Phone: **(323) 264-4151**
  - Note: For urgent law enforcement matters. For immediate life-threatening emergencies, dial 911. Services City of Commerce, Cudahy, Maywood, and unincorporated areas like Belvedere Gardens, City Terrace, Eastmont, East Los Angeles, Saybrook Park, Union Pacific.

- **Los Angeles County Fire Department Station 3 (Direct Line):**

  - Phone: **(323) 269-4124**
  - Note: For direct station inquiries regarding ambulance & fire services. For immediate fire or medical emergencies, dial 911.

- **CHP Traffic Accident Response (South Gate-East LA):**

  - Phone: **(323) 980-6400**
  - Note: For reporting or inquiring about traffic accidents.

- **Animal Control (Downey Shelter - Urgent Animal Issues):**

  - Phone: **(562) 940-6898**
  - Note: For animal-related emergencies or services.

- **South Coast AQMD (Air Quality Complaints):**

  - Phone: **1-800-CUT SMOG (1-800-288-7664)**
  - Note: To file a complaint about smog, strong odors, or other air quality issues.

- **Road Closures & Hazards (24-Hour Hotline):**

  - Phone: **1 (800) 675-4357**
  - Note: For 24-hour information on road closures or to report road hazards.
  - Other Inquiries (Non-24hr): (323) 261-2160
