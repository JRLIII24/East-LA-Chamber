Garbage, recycling, and green waste collection services in unincorporated areas of East Los Angeles are provided by private waste hauling companies under agreement with Los Angeles County Public Works. Schedules and specific services depend on your assigned hauler.

### How to Find Your Collection Schedule
1. **Identify Your Waste Hauler:**

  - Visit the LA County Public Works website: [pw.lacounty.gov](https://pw.lacounty.gov/). Navigate to sections related to waste management, "Residential Franchise System," "Garbage Disposal Districts," or look for a "Who Picks Up My Trash?" lookup tool.
  - The specific link for franchise areas is often around [pw.lacounty.gov/epd/Franchise/](https://pw.lacounty.gov/epd/Franchise/).

2. **Visit Your Hauler's Website:** Once you know your hauler (e.g., Republic Services, Waste Management, etc., depending on the specific zone in East LA), visit their official website. Most haulers provide:

  - Online schedule lookup by address.
  - Printable collection calendars.
  - Information on holiday schedule adjustments.
  - Guidelines for cart placement and accepted materials.

### General Collection Information
- **Frequency:** Typically, trash, recycling, and green waste are collected once per week on a designated day.
- **Cart Placement:**

  - Place carts at the curb by the time specified by your hauler (often 6:00 AM or 7:00 AM) on your collection day.
  - Ensure lids are closed.
  - Wheels should generally face your home.
  - Allow a few feet of clearance between carts and any obstructions like parked cars, mailboxes, or trees.

- **Holiday Delays:** Collection is usually delayed by one day if your regular pickup day falls on or after major holidays such as:

  - New Year's Day
  - Memorial Day
  - Independence Day (July 4th)
  - Labor Day
  - Thanksgiving Day
  - Christmas Day
  - *Always confirm your hauler's specific holiday schedule.*

### Missed Pickups
- If your carts were placed correctly and on time but not serviced, contact your waste hauler directly to report the missed pickup. Their contact information will be on their website or your utility bill.

### Bulky Item Pickup
- Most haulers offer free collection of bulky items (furniture, appliances, etc.) a few times per year. This service usually requires you to schedule a pickup in advance. Check your hauler’s website for details on how to request this service and what items are accepted.

*For the most accurate and personalized service information, always refer to the official website or contact customer service of the waste hauler assigned to your specific address in East Los Angeles.*
